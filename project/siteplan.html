Site Name

Packers Central

I chose the name Packers Central because this website will be a central location for Green Bay Packers fans to find tickets, team information, and other Packers related content. As someone from Green Bay, Wisconsin, and a lifelong Packers fan, I wanted to create a website that represents my interest in the team.

Proposed domain: packerscentral.com

Site Purpose

The purpose of Packers Central is to provide Green Bay Packers fans with a convenient place to find information. The website will include a homepage featuring Packers content, an informational page about the team's history and background, and a ticket page with a form where visitors can enter their information and submit a ticket request.

Scenarios

  • Where can I find information about the Green Bay Packers and their history?
  • How can I request tickets to an upcoming Packers game?
  • Where can I learn more about the Packers' home stadium?
  • What information should I know before attending a Packers game?

Color Schema

The website will use colors inspired by the Green Bay Packers team colors. Green and gold will be the primary colors throughout the website.

Packers Green: #203731

Used for headers, navigation, and section backgrounds.

Packers Gold: #FFB612

Used for accents, buttons, borders, and highlights.

White: #FFFFFF

Used for the main background and text on dark backgrounds.

Typography

The website will use two fonts to create a clean, readable design that also has a sporty appearance.

Oswald - Headings

Oswald will be used for headings and titles because its bold, athletic appearance fits the football theme.

Roboto - Paragraphs and Body Text

Roboto will be used for paragraphs, navigation links, and general website content because it is easy to read.
